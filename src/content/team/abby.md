bio_en: >-
  Abby is a missionary kid, born and raised in South Korea. Her parents serve
  full-time with Word of Life, so she grew up knowing the name of Jesus as
  naturally as she knew "mom" and "dad." From an early age, she loved reading
  missionary stories and found herself wondering: "Why would they spend their
  whole lives telling Jesus' story? Was it really that special?" Through her
  mother's explanation, she accepted Jesus' gift of salvation around age six.


  But assurance didn't come easily. When her family moved to Jeju in 2009 to start WOLBI Jeju, she watched students and campers respond to the gospel year after year — while quietly wondering, "Am I really saved? I don't feel anything. What if I didn't do it right? I don't want to go to hell!" She raised her hand multiple times hoping to find security, but the relief was always temporary.


  Then one night, her mother shared a simple truth that changed everything: Jesus paid for all her sins — past, present, and future. Her salvation wasn't about what she had done or not done; it was about what Jesus had done. When she stands before God someday, she won't say "Let me in because I did x, y, z" — she'll say "Because You promised, and I believe You." Since that night, Abby has had no doubt: she is saved.
