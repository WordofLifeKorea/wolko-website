  My name is Peter. I was born into a third-generation Presbyterian family. During my childhood I was blessed to attend early morning prayer meetings with my mother.   저는 Peter입니다. 3대째 신앙을 이어 온 장로교 가정에서 태어났습니다. 어린 시절에는 어머니와 함께 새벽기도를 드리는 복된 환경 속에서 자랐습니다. 어머니는 항상 "하나님의 영광을 위해 더 친절한 사람, 더 나은 사람이 되어라"라고 당부하셨고, 저도 그 말씀대로 살기 위해 노력하였습니다.
  
  그러나 청소년기에 접어들며 수많은 고민과 의문이 밀려왔습니다. 아무리 애써도 마음은 늘 공허했고 삶의 목적을 알 수 없었습니다. 부모님을 실망시키지 않으려 이중적인 삶을 살았고, 결국 완벽한 삶을 살아가는 것에 지쳐 극단적인 선택을 시도하였습니다. 그 시도는 실패로 끝났고, 이후 무거운 마음을 안고 하루하루를 보냈습니다.
  
  고등학교 1학년 여름, 하나님의 뜻을 찾고자 간절한 마음으로 한 수련회에 참석하였습니다. 하나님께서 그 기도에 응답하셨습니다. 강사님의 메시지를 통해 제가 죄로 인해 마땅히 죽어야 할 존재이며 예수 그리스도가 필요하다는 것을 처음으로 깨달았습니다. 강사님은 요한일서 1장 9절을 통해 말씀하셨습니다. bio_en_2: |-
  My name is Miriam. I was born into a non-believing family. As a child I occasionally attended a nearby church, but I did not know who Jesus was. The middle school I attended was a Christian private school. There was a church in the school, so non-believing students had to attend chapel services to receive credit. I went to church and participated in religious activities simply for the grades.
  
  In the eleventh grade I attended a retreat for Christian leaders at the Korea Christian Conference Ground (KCCG) in Bulgwang-dong, Seoul, and that summer I met Jesus. "Truly, truly, I say to you, whoever hears my word and believes him who sent me has eternal life. He does not come into judgment, but has passed from death to life." (John 5:24)
  
  After graduating from high school, I served at the KCCG, helping with youth ministry, and came to recognize that this was the vision God had given me. I met my husband, who shared that same vision, and together we committed to building the kingdom of God. We then joined Word of Life Korea, which reaches teenagers with the gospel through camps, and have continued serving the Lord together to this day. Our deepest longing is to see many young people in South Korea hear the gospel through the lives and testimonies of their Christian friends.
