bio_en: "Javaris was saved in November 2017 during his senior year of high school. The year before, he had transferred to an international Christian school — and that move was God's way of pulling him back into the Christian life. In his junior year, he had the opportunity to go on a mission trip to the Philippines. That trip was a huge eye-opener on what Christians are really meant to do and how they should be serving others. In the fall semester of his senior year, guests from Voice of the Martyrs spoke about how all Christians should be living their lives for the Lord, and Javaris raised his hand when the pastor invited students to make the decision themselves. From that moment, he wanted to learn more about God and live according to the plan He had for him. He went on to study at Moody Bible Institute before joining the WOLBI Jeju team in South Korea, hoping to see many young people come to know the Lord personally and become strong disciple-makers for Jesus."
