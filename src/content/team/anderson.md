bio_en: When I was just seven years old, the terrifying reality of hell gripped

  York. One day I went down to the edge of the lake to be alone with God. There,
  I examined my heart, repented of my sin, and put my faith fully in Jesus
  Christ. From that moment on, my life was transformed by His grace. Chan-Joo
  grew up in a Christian family but went through confusing times in college.
  Through the book of Hosea during SYME, she understood the depth of the gospel
  and received Jesus as her Savior. Together they minister with the hope that
  young Koreans will truly understand Christianity and share the gospel.
