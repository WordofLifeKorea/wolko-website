bio_en: |-
  I grew up in a Christian household and never doubted that Jesus is the only way to salvation. However, even after dedicating my life to Jesus as a teenager, I continually struggled with the fear that God was waiting for me to make a mistake. That fear transformed into terror of being excluded from God's grace, and over time it seeped into every area of my life, making me sick, lonely, robbing me of passion, and leaving me purposeless despite being a believer. But as He always does, God had a different plan.
  
  God wrapped me in a vibrant Christian community where I came to understand just how purposeful and joyful a life in Christ should be. And through that new environment, God completely transformed the way I understood the Gospel. Suddenly, I realized this wasn't about my own perfection—it was about Christ's perfection. The Gospel truly is good news!
  
  Ultimately, God gave me courage to go abroad and serve as a short-term missionary in Word of Life Korea's Jr. SYME program. There, God revealed to me the true meaning of Paul's words: "when I am weak, then I am strong" (2 Corinthians 12:10). I resolved that if God could use me to transform the hearts of other young believers walking a similar path of faith, I would gladly serve my whole life in weakness. In short, God awakened something new within me—not the fear I once knew, but a longing to help lead Korea's next generation of young people into the true joy, passion, and purpose of the Gospel.
