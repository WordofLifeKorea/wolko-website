bio_en: |-
  Hi, my name is HwanKyu Kim. I was born and raised in South Korea. My mother had a personal relationship with Christ, but my father grew up with a Buddhist influence. My whole family attended church every Sunday — the only reason my father came was that it had been a condition my mother's parents set before accepting their marriage. For me, church was simply a place I had to go on Sundays whether I wanted to or not.
  
  I had no negative feelings toward church. I believed in heaven and hell, and through Sunday school I was familiar with the Bible, God, and Jesus Christ — but I had no real interest in coming to know Christ personally. My mom had me serve on the choir and ministry teams. At that time I thought there were two ways of salvation: salvation through good works, and salvation through my mother's sincere faith. Assurance of salvation was always unclear to me, and I had no personal relationship with Christ.
  
  In South Korea, all men must serve in the military for about two years. I joined the army at twenty-one, and during my service I continued attending church every Sunday — though most of my fellow soldiers went only to receive snacks or gifts. After finishing my service I had a few months off, and my mother introduced me to a place called SYME (School of Youth Ministry in English). I was drawn to it because it offered a chance to develop my English — something that could open doors to better opportunities. My father was not pleased at first, but he allowed me to go for the English benefit.
  
  It was there that a missionary shared the gospel with me, and for the first time I truly understood how I could have assurance of salvation. I came to see that Christ had died and risen to make a way for me to have a personal relationship with God — and that this was personal. Salvation is not something earned; it is a free gift. After being born again through the gospel of Christ, I decided to serve the Lord throughout my life. I enrolled at the Word of Life Bible Institute, and around that time my father also came to know Christ — he could not have been more supportive or more joyful about my decision. I studied at the Bible Institute in 2016 and 2017. In 2018 my father passed away from cancer, but I can now clearly see how God directed his life and mine throughout it all.
  
  Since then, the Lord has done many great things in my life. Now I am serving Him in South Korea, and I am filled with joy to be used by God.
