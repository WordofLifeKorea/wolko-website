bio_en: |-
  I grew up in a family that attended church every week. Since I had grown up going to church every week, going to church was simply a normal part of my daily life. I was actively involved in church throughout my school years, but I did not truly understand what it meant to have a "personal relationship with God."

  I thought that because I was a "sinner" and God was the "Creator and a perfect God," there was an unbridgeable wall between God, who is perfect, and me, a sinner. I also thought that I went to church, read the Bible, and worshiped simply because those were things I was supposed to do as a Christian.

  However, God led me to WOLBI JEJU, and as I studied the Bible there, my perspective began to change. I realized that through the Bible, I can know who God is; through His living Word, I can experience God speaking to me and answering me; and prayer is a time when I communicate with God.

  I also truly came to understand that God desired to have a relationship with me, a sinner, and that He made a way between God and me by sending His beloved Son, Jesus, to die on the cross and rise again.

  Because of this, I came to understand that having a personal relationship with God is not simply a religious expression. Just as we build a relationship with a friend by spending time together and getting to know each other, I learned that I can grow closer to the Lord by spending time with Him through His Word, where He reveals Himself to me.

  The more I came to know the Lord, the more I desired Him. The emptiness in my heart that nothing in this world could fill began to be filled, and I began to discover purpose in my life.

  Even today, there are many young people and teenagers who attend church but do not truly understand what it means to have a relationship with God. There are also many who faithfully attend church but continue to live with an emptiness that nothing seems to fill.

  Because of this, I wanted to share with them the meaning of the Gospel and the significance of Jesus' death on the cross. In order to serve these young people and teenagers, I decided to serve at WOLBI JEJU.
